  • The regional SacSewer treatment charge appears on the same bill but is set by SacSewer — a separate regional agency — and is excluded from this calculator. New SacSewer treatment rates take effect July 1, 2026: $47.00 per month for a single-family house and $35.25 per month for each multi-family unit. For questions about how your property is classified, contact SacSewer at (916) 875-5500 or visit sacsewer.com.
  • Commercial wastewater (current): a readiness-to-serve charge by meter size that covers usage up to the meter-size threshold, plus $1.2953/CCF on usage above that threshold. Proposed: a fixed meter charge plus the proposed per-CCF rate for that year on usage above the meter-size threshold. SacSewer treatment is excluded.
  • Storm drainage: both the 1996 room-based fee and the 2022 parcel fee are shown. Commercial storm fees use gross parcel sq ft and property type. Neither fee is proposed to change in this rate study.
  • Solid waste & street sweeping are flat (effective Jan 1, 2024) and held constant across all years, as there are no current plans for adjustments.

Find these on your bill

Each highlighted field below matches an input in the calculator. Circled numbers correspond to the legend.

  • 1Meter size — found in the meter table. Sets your fixed water charge.
  • 2Water usage — enter the cubic feet figure from your bill and set the units toggle to "Cubic feet," or divide by 100 and enter CCF.
  • 3Number of rooms — sets the current wastewater and 1996 storm drainage tier.
  • 4Storm tier — "SFR1," "SFR2," or "SFR3" on the 2022 storm drainage line. SFR2 is most common.
  • 5Garbage container size — bill shows "30-gallon"; select 32-gallon in the calculator (same container, same rate).
  • 6Total garbage containers — the quantity shown before the container size on the garbage line.
  • 1Meter size — in the meter table for each meter section. Add one calculator row per meter.
  • 2Water usage — enter the cubic feet figure from your bill and set the units toggle to "Cubic feet," or divide by 100 and enter CCF. The same reading also drives the wastewater volume charge.
  • 3Meter type — if a meter section has no wastewater lines, it is an irrigation meter. Select "Irrigation (no wastewater charge)" for that row.
